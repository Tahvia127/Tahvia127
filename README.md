## Hi, I'm La'Tahvia Williams 👋

I build data tools for public-interest work — and I care as much about how the result *reads* as how it computes.

Right now I'm at the **University of Chicago**, studying data science, and most of my work sits where policy questions meet messy real-world data: conflict data, sentencing records, census tracts, healthcare payments. The through-line is using data to make something invisible visible.

---

### 🔭 What I'm building

**[Rights Within Reach](https://github.com/Tahvia127/rights-within-reach)** — a working demo of free, plain-language legal information for Illinois residents, in five languages and read aloud. Retrieval-augmented over 130+ verified legal-aid sources, with confidence ratings and hard refusals on out-of-scope questions. In active development; expanding beyond Illinois.
`Python` `FastAPI` `React` `Chroma` `Claude`

**[PublicPath](https://github.com/Tahvia127/PublicPath)** — a single searchable index of public-interest jobs, aggregated daily from 20+ federal, state, city, nonprofit, and fellowship sources into one Supabase-backed platform with a personalized weekly digest.
`Python` `Supabase` `Postgres` `JavaScript`

### 📊 Selected work

| Project | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **[Forgotten Wars](https://github.com/Tahvia127/data-237-final-project)** | Does global attention follow human suffering? 884,216 conflict events vs. search volume — correlation ranges from r=0.77 to r=0.03, independent of death toll. |
| **[Words and Money at ECOSOC](https://github.com/Tahvia127/BUSN-20800-Final-Project-)** | Do the UN's stated priorities match where its money flows? Text-as-data across 26 years of transcripts. |
